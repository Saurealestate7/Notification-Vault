Notification Vault — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026  ·  Version 1.4

1. What we collect

Notification Vault reads the title, text, source app name, package identifier, and timestamp of notifications from apps you choose to track. You control exactly which apps are tracked, in the app's Apps screen. Notifications from apps you have not selected are never read or stored.

2. Where your data is stored

All notification data is stored only in a local database on your device. Notification Vault has no backend server of its own and no cloud sync. Your notification content never leaves your device.

3. The two exceptions

Notification Vault uses two third-party services, each for a purpose unrelated to your notification content:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash diagnostics) — collects crash details such as exception type and stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, and installation identifiers used to associate reports from the same app installation. We use this information to find and fix technical problems. We never deliberately attach notification titles, notification text, tracked-app names, or tracked package identifiers to Crashlytics reports. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier, basic device/app information, and subscription events where applicable, to load subscription offerings and manage entitlements. This happens each time the app is opened, not only if you purchase a subscription. RevenueCat never has access to your captured notifications. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4. Data retention and deletion

Automatic retention: By default, Notification Vault does not automatically delete notifications. Notifications are kept on your device until you delete them yourself.

Manual deletion: You can delete individual notifications or a selection of notifications from the Vault. Storage Management provides controls for clearing all notifications, all tracked-app preferences, or all local app data at once. Where the app displays an Undo action, deletion can be reversed during that brief Undo window; afterward it is permanent.

Data held by third parties: the in-app deletion controls above cover data stored on your device. They do not reach the two off-device flows described in Section 3 — crash reports already received by Firebase Crashlytics, or the anonymous customer record and subscription history held by RevenueCat and Google Play. Some of those subscription and payment records are retained by Google and RevenueCat under their own legal and accounting obligations and cannot be deleted on request. For anything that can be deleted or anonymised, email us at the address in Section 8 and we will action what we are able to, or point you to the relevant provider.

What a support email contains: when you use the in-app "Contact Support" action, the app pre-fills the message with technical details to help us help you — your app version, Android version, device model, and a billing support ID. The billing support ID is a random, pseudonymous identifier linked to your RevenueCat customer record. It is not an account username and does not directly contain your name or email address. We use it to locate your RevenueCat customer record and process deletion or anonymisation requests where permitted — see the paragraph above for what this can and cannot reach. This does not cancel your subscription; to cancel or manage a subscription, use Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions), not this email. Nothing else is added automatically to the email, and no notification content is ever included. The email is only sent if you choose to send it.

5. Data we do not collect

Notification Vault has no account or registration system, and we do not collect your name, email address, or phone number through the app automatically. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, solely to respond to your support, privacy, or deletion request. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or advertising identifiers.

6. Children's privacy

Notification Vault is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
